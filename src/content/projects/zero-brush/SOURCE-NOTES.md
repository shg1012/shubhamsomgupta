# Zero Brush source and evidence notes

This file records what the published case study can support, what was confirmed directly by Shubham, and what remains absent or unvalidated. It is intentionally separate from the public narrative.

## Confirmed from supplied project resources

- Academic Design Thinking context at ISDI.
- Four-person team: Pooja Doshi, Anna Abraham, Raj Vichare, and Shubham Som Gupta.
- Academic mentors shown on the project panel: Amit Kundal and Deepak Singh.
- Early project identity: S.T.R.I. — Sustainable, Trackable, Replaceable, and Incentivised.
- Mumbai urban-waste starting point, campus cleaning-staff observation and shadowing, and conversations with garbage collectors.
- Stakeholder, empathy, affinity, ideation, concept-selection, physical-model, CAD, rendering, and video artifacts.
- 39 ideas in `Idea Dump.xlsx`; four concepts in the team DVF evaluation.
- Two prototyping phases and exactly two visible formative test participants.
- Documented test issues: head wobble, body/slider ergonomics, and multi-pellet dispensing.
- Only one verified corrupt source asset: `Final 3D Model/Renders/brush.61.png` at zero bytes.

## Confirmed directly by Shubham

- Project timing: July–December 2019, six months.
- Personal role and contribution statement used in the frontmatter.
- Full consent to publish the named team, identifiable testing images, and video stills.
- The head-attachment issue was addressed by improving tolerance between head and handle.
- The slider problem was addressed through a revised slider mechanism.
- The multi-pellet dispensing issue remained unresolved.
- The bamboo head, mixed bristles, toothpaste-pellet models, and 3D-printed body were physically fabricated and tested.
- Testing did not validate durability.
- Approved portfolio quote and attribution: “I want my kid to brush twice a day, but I don’t do it myself.” — Susan, homemaker and mother.

## Missing or unsupported

- A defensible total count for research interviews or participants beyond the two prototype testers.
- A completed service blueprint or project-specific customer journey. The only explicit journey-map file found was a blank external template.
- Formal measured post-fix validation of the revised head tolerance and slider.
- Durability, clinical efficacy, ingredient safety, hygiene performance, long-term habit change, lifecycle benefit, biodegradability, quantified waste reduction, manufacturing feasibility, cost, adoption, sales, or commercial impact.
- One coherent final production material specification; the archive contains changing and inconsistent material proposals.
- Resolution of the multi-pellet dispensing issue.
- Publication consent is not documented for identifiable field workers or bystanders; the field-observation photograph was excluded from the published case study.

## Content excluded from the public case study

- Unsupported numerical environmental and waste claims.
- External Nielsen Norman Group and MindTools PDFs and templates.
- Freepik assets, downloaded reference models, and licence-unclear stock imagery.
- Colgate, medicine-bottle, toothbrush, holder, and other third-party reference CAD.
- Lifestyle composites whose background-image licences could not be verified.
- The corrupt `brush.61.png` render.
- An unrelated photography empathy-map screenshot and other non-project presentation content.
- The field-observation photograph containing identifiable bystanders and background contact details.
