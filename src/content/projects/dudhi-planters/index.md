## Overview

**Planter Design: Dudhi Industries** was a client project delivered from January to April 2019. As sole designer, I developed the planter collection alongside the Dudhi brand identity and sales-marketing materials.

The work moved from product and concept exploration through R&D with 3D-printed prototypes, then into a manufactured and sold collection. The catalogue below is the released sales material. It is presented in its original numeric sequence.
