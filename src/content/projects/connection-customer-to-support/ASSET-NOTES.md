# ISPM customer-support asset notes

The generated `images/hero.webp` is an original, project-safe conceptual visual.

Everything under `images/source-evidence/` is a lossless, metadata-free WebP conversion of an embedded image from `Case Study 1.pptx`. These files are temporary evidence assets and should be replaced before the next public deployment.

## Replacement risks

- Legacy company and product branding appears in several interface screenshots.
- Support email addresses, phone numbers, account names, and internal configuration values are visible.
- `implemented-email-workflow.webp` contains a personal name and email address.
- Research collages contain third-party product examples whose public reuse rights have not been verified.
